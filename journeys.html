Three ways to wander

Each idea is a starting point, not a strict schedule. Add a rest day or follow a recommendation from a local.

01

Highlands / 3–5 days

Follow the mountain paths

Base yourself near Sa Pa, walk between villages with a local guide, and save an unhurried morning for the changing light across the terraces.

  • Best for: walking and wide-open views
  • Leave room for: village markets and tea stops
Learn about local craft ↗
02

Coast / 2–4 days

Let the water set the pace

Watch the bay wake up, take a small boat into quieter corners, and trade a packed itinerary for a long look at the horizon.

  • Best for: water, boats, and slow mornings
  • Leave room for: a sunset without a plan
Explore more places ↗
03

Heritage / 2–3 days

Stay after the day-trippers

Explore old lanes early, rest through the warmest hours, and return at dusk when the river, lanterns, and neighbourhood life take over.

  • Best for: architecture and local food
  • Leave room for: a second walk through town

A note on travelling well

Be a guest, not just a visitor.

Ask before photographing people, choose local guides when possible, take reusable essentials, and follow community guidance around nature and heritage sites.
